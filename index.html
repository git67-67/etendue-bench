    Rules of thumb from the numbers

      Method: 3D skew-ray trace of the rotationally symmetric system with Snell refraction, unpolarised reflection loss (Fresnel equations) at every glass surface, total internal reflection and mirror absorption. The diagram shows one cross-section of those rays. Brightness is read at the wall as the average over a small central disc, and never above the etendue limit. Sun reference: 100 klx, 0.53° across, 1.6 Gcd/m².
