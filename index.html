    Rules of thumb from the numbers

      Method: meridional ray trace with Snell refraction and unpolarised reflection loss (Fresnel equations) at every glass surface. Efficiency, lux and luminance use a round LED and round lenses, estimated from the same rays. Sun reference: 100 klx, 0.53° across, 1.6 Gcd/m².
